# Problem Statement: Wishlist-to-Purchase Conversion

## 1. Knowns

### Business Metric
* **Wishlist-to-purchase conversion rate:** The percentage of users who purchase at least one item from their wishlist within 30 days of adding it.

### Business Goal
* **Target:** Significantly increase the wishlist-to-purchase conversion rate within 30 days of addition.

### Wishlist Behavior
* Users actively save items to their wishlist, showing explicit interest in specific products.
* Only a small fraction of these saved items are purchased.

### Critical Constraint
* **No Direct Monetary Incentives:** We cannot offer discounts, coupons, cashbacks, sales, or other monetary rewards to encourage purchases. The solution must be non-monetary (e.g., product-led, experience-led, or info-led).

---

## 2. Unknowns

### Underlying User Problem
* The root reason(s) why users add items to their wishlist but fail to purchase them is **unknown**.
* We do not assume it is due to price, fit, size, styling, reviews, social proof, lack of discounts, lack of reminders, or comparison friction. These are merely potential hypotheses to be evaluated.

### Major Purchase Barriers
* The barriers, uncertainties, or friction points that occur after wishlisting are unidentified.
* We do not know what prevents users from taking the final step to purchase.

### Relevant User Segments
* It is unknown how wishlist-to-purchase behaviors, motivations, and pain points differ across various user cohorts (e.g., gender, shopping frequency, fashion categories, budget vs. premium buyers, new vs. returning users).

### Opportunity Areas
* We have not yet identified, quantified, or prioritized specific opportunity areas where changes to the product or user experience could address user friction.

---

## 3. Research Objective

Our primary research objective is to:
**Discover, validate, and define the underlying user problems and purchase barriers that prevent wishlist conversion, and identify prioritized opportunity areas for non-monetary product interventions.**

We will achieve this through a mixed-methods research approach:
1. **Secondary Research & AI Analysis:** Process large-scale public user feedback using the AI Discovery Engine to identify common themes, purchase barriers, and customer frustrations in fashion e-commerce.
2. **Primary Research:** Conduct 5–6 in-depth user interviews with active Myntra users who frequently wishlist items but rarely purchase them to validate the problems identified.
3. **Synthesis & Validation:** Map the findings to a validated problem definition, which will serve as the foundation for our Minimum Viable Product (MVP) design.
